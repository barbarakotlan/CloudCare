# Legal and compliance review (adversarial)

Reviewed 8 November 2026 against the code on `jacob/frontend` (HEAD `b1a2b7a` plus the working tree). The question asked throughout: if a patient, a regulator, a carrier, or a vendor's trust-and-safety team read our pages and listened to our calls, where would they say we promised something we do not do, or did something we had no right to do?

This is not legal advice. It is an engineering-side issue list to hand to counsel. Every law or vendor term below links to the page it was checked against; anything that could not be confirmed is marked "verify".

**Severity** is given twice: for the hackathon demo as it runs today (fictional patient, public demo accounts, but open sign-up and a live phone number), and for use with real patients. "Blocker" means do not invite real patients until it is resolved.

**Status:** *Fixed* = changed in this pass (Terms, Privacy, `components/legal`). *Copy listed* = replacement text is in the "Replacement text for other owners" section below. *Open* = needs code, configuration, a contract, or an owner/counsel decision.

| L1 | **High / Blocker** | **Anyone can make Foggy call a stranger.** The demo patient account is public (credentials on the login page). Any visitor can save any +1 number on it and press "Call me". There is no number verification (`phone_verified_at` unused), no demo-account guard on `/outreach/*`, and the only limit is one call per 2 minutes (up to ~30 AI-voice calls an hour to the same victim). An AI-generated voice is an "artificial" voice under the TCPA (FCC 24-17), and calls to a mobile number with an artificial voice need the called party's prior express consent (47 CFR 64.1200(a)(1)(iii)); the person who pressed the button is not the called party. Statutory damages are per call (47 U.S.C. 227(b)(3)). Also an abuse/harassment vector and a carrier-reputation risk for the toll-free number. | (1) Block `PATCH /patients/me` phone changes and `/outreach/*` for `@demo.com` accounts, or allow only numbers on an env allowlist (`OUTREACH_DEMO_ALLOWED_NUMBERS`). (2) One-time-code verification of the number before the first call or text. (3) Daily cap (for example 3 calls/day, 1 text/day). (4) Have the call opening say the patient asked for it, so a wrong person can say so and hang up. | Partly mitigated in the working tree by the security pass (uncommitted at the time of this review): `backend/app/outreach/guard.py` adds a 10-minute cooldown after a number changes, a per-number daily cap (6), a per-patient daily cap, and a server-wide hourly call cap.
